## 1. Delivery and Installation Policy

Every Sthuthi mandir is delivered and installed by our team at no extra charge anywhere in India, on a date agreed in your Order Confirmation.

### Where we deliver

We deliver to all serviceable PIN codes in India. Remote or restricted-access locations may need a longer schedule, which we confirm before you pay the design fee.

### Timelines

- Your delivery and installation date is set in the Order Confirmation once you approve the drawings.
- Production lead time is 10–16 weeks for cabinets and 18–28 weeks for walk-in cabins, counted from drawing approval and receipt of the production advance.
- Festival and griha pravesh dates cannot be guaranteed unless they are written into your Order Confirmation.
- Delays caused by changes you request, a site that is not ready, or events beyond our control (natural disasters, transport strikes, government restrictions) extend the date by the time lost.

### Before installation: your site checklist

You are responsible for having the site ready on the agreed date:

1. The room is clear, dry, finished (paint, flooring and electrical work complete) and free of other trades.
2. The access route from the vehicle to the room, including lift, stairs, doors and corridors, can take the modules listed in your Order Confirmation.
3. A working power point is available near the mandir position.
4. For every walk-in cabin, and any cabinet over 300 kg, a structural engineer has signed off that the floor can carry the stated weight. We can arrange the engineer at cost if you prefer.
5. Society or building permissions for delivery, lift use and work hours are in place.

### On installation day

- Our team unpacks, assembles, levels, anchors and cleans the mandir, places the idols and sets out the pooja items.
- We photograph the crates before opening and the mandir after installation.
- You or your representative inspect the mandir with us and sign the Handover Certificate. Note any visible issue on the certificate.

### If the site is not ready

If installation cannot go ahead because the site checklist is not met, we reschedule to the next available slot. Extra travel, labour and transport costs for the failed visit are charged at actuals. If you ask us to hold a finished mandir for more than 30 days past the agreed date, storage and insurance are charged at actual cost.

### Transit risk

The mandir is insured in transit and stays at Sthuthi's risk until the Handover Certificate is signed. Any damage before handover is ours to repair or replace.

### Safe use

Place lit diyas only on the non-combustible diya plate supplied, keep flames at least 45 cm below any wood surface, and never leave a lit lamp unattended. Use the flame-free lamp option in closed cabins where ventilation is limited.

## 4. Warranty Policy

Every Sthuthi mandir carries a warranty against manufacturing and installation defects, starting on the date of the Handover Certificate.

### What is covered, and for how long

| Part | Covered against | Period |
|---|---|---|
| Structure and joinery | Cracks, splits, warping or loosening joints from material or workmanship faults | 3 years |
| Carving, stone and marble | Breakage or cracking not caused by impact | 3 years |
| Finishes, gilding and plating | Peeling, blistering or discolouration under normal use | 1 year |
| Idols | Casting flaws, stone faults and paint lifting | 1 year |
| Doors, hinges, locks and hardware | Failure in normal use | 1 year |
| Integrated lighting and wiring | Failure in normal use | 1 year |
| Installation workmanship | Levelling, anchoring and assembly faults | 1 year |

### What we do

We inspect the defect, then repair it or replace the affected part at our cost, including parts, labour and travel. If neither is possible, we refund the value of the affected part.

### What is not covered

- Natural variation in wood grain, colour, stone veining and hand-carved detail
- Damage from water, damp walls, direct sunlight, pests or humidity outside normal indoor conditions
- Heat, smoke or burn marks from lamps, incense or camphor
- Impact, misuse, or cleaning with harsh chemicals or abrasives
- Moving, altering or reinstalling the mandir by anyone other than Sthuthi
- Normal wear of plated and gilded surfaces from daily ritual use

### How to claim

Send your order number, a description and photos on WhatsApp or by phone to +91 8951618186. We respond within 2 working days and arrange an inspection within 10 working days.

### Care

Wipe wood and metal with a soft dry cloth, keep the mandir away from damp walls, and use the supplied diya plate for every lamp. A care guide comes with every handover.
